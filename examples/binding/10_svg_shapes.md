# 10 · SVG shapes with binding

The SVG of HTML / SVG example 08, with six attributes turned into `^` pointers.

- The ranges change the rectangle width, the circle radius, the ellipse height and one `stroke_width` shared by the line and the polyline.
- The color field paints the triangle; the text field rewrites the label.
- Every control has `live` true, so the drawing follows while you drag or type.

**Try it:** Bind the circle's `cx` to a new range and move the circle along the row.
